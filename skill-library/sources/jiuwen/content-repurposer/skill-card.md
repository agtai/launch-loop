## Description: <br>
Content Repurposer turns one source, such as a blog post, transcript, newsletter, or idea, into platform-native drafts for X/Twitter, LinkedIn, newsletters, Instagram or LinkedIn carousels, and short-form video. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Creators, marketers, and content teams use this skill to repurpose a single source into a ready-to-post multi-platform distribution pack with hooks, formatting, CTAs, and cadence guidance. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated drafts may include incorrect examples, assumed statistics, or platform claims if the source material is thin. <br>
Mitigation: Review all drafts before publishing and replace any text marked as assumed. <br>
Risk: The linked ContentGoldMine project is external to this skill and is not part of the installed skill behavior. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the external automation link as optional and evaluate it separately before use. <br>
